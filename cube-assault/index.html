NEW IN v1.7.2

    Patch notes

    v1.7.2 Climb the town

    • Every building in Frontier's town is now hollow and open. Walk in through the door and take the stairs up floor by floor to the roof, where a low wall gives you cover for sniping.
    • Cubes follow you. They walk in through the door, climb the stairs and come up after you, and if you jump down they take the stairs back down. Even the King squeezes up. Blue Cubes still prefer to shoot at you from the street when they can see you.
    • Outside, cubes now walk round the town's buildings instead of bumping into the walls.
    • New in the Shop: the Combat Knife (40 coins). Hold 7 to pull it out and keep slashing. Every slash hits every cube in front of you within arm's reach.
    • A What's New pop-up now opens the first time you load each new version.
    • Fix: holding Shift to sprint stopped the number keys from switching guns.
    • Fix: a big hit could knock a cube straight through a thin wall.
    • Fix: the "New guns unlocked" banner could pop up in your next game if you quit right after reaching wave 5.
    • Fix: health packs, the turret and the King's shockwave now work at the height they happen, not always at ground level.
    • Fix: cubes could spawn inside a house or a mountain and get stuck there.

    v1.7.1 Turret & fixes

    • New in the Shop: the Turret (400 coins). Press 6 in a game to set it down next to you. It shoots the nearest cube it can see once a second, and any normal cube goes down in two hits. The King only takes a scratch from each shot.
    • Your turret has 200 HP, double yours, and never heals. Cubes will go for it if it's closer than you, and enemy shots and the King's shockwave hurt it. Its health shows above yours, and it appears on the radar. You can only have one out at a time.
    • Fix: from inside a house, the ceiling showed the roof tiles stretched into a spiderweb pattern. Houses now have proper wooden ceilings.
    • Fix: the top of every doorway flickered where the door frame met the wall.
    • Windows now show on the inside walls of houses too, not just outside.
    • Fix: window bars flickered against the glass from a distance, and on Frontier the town's streets flickered against the paving.

    v1.7 Frontier

    • Pressing Play now lets you choose a map: Classic Village (the original map) or Frontier.
    • Frontier (work in progress) is four times the size of the classic map. It has the village in the middle, a dense forest, a mountain range, a town of tall buildings and parked cars, and a farm with a barn and fenced fields.
    • You always start in the middle of the village, on both maps.
    • On Frontier, cubes spawn 30 to 50 metres from you instead of at the outer wall, so you're not waiting for them to cross the map.

    v1.6.3 Pop!

    • The Shotgun and Sniper now unlock when you reach wave 5 in every game, instead of staying unlocked forever. Every game starts with the Pistol and Rifle, plus the Extreme Gun if you've bought it.
    • New sounds: destroyed cubes now burst with a punchy pop and rattling debris, deeper for bigger cubes. Every gun also has its own sound when you switch to it.
    • Fix: the snow on the mountains flickered and had ragged streaks, because it sat exactly on the rock.
    • Fix: clouds drifted straight through the mountains and jumped from one side of the sky to the other. They now circle slowly over the village.

    v1.6.2 Fresh start

    • You now start with the Pistol and Rifle. Reach wave 5 to unlock the Shotgun and Sniper for good. If you've already reached wave 5, they're already unlocked.
    • New in Settings: Reset all data. After a warning screen, it wipes your name, level, stats, coins, guns and settings and starts the game fresh.
    • Fix: if your browser blocked mouse capture, the message telling you what to do never appeared. It now shows at the bottom of the screen.
    • Fix: the kill-combo counter stayed on screen behind the game-over screen.

    v1.6.1 Fixes & polish

    • The Extreme Gun now costs 1,000 coins (was 100). If you already own it, you keep it.
    • Fix: a big hit near the edge of the map could knock a cube through the outer wall, where it got stuck and the wave never ended.
    • Fix: destroyed cubes and enemy energy shots weren't cleared from memory, so long sessions slowly got slower.
    • Your best wave now counts when you quit to the menu, not only when you die.
    • The new-wave banner now shows how much health you actually got back, or "Full health".

    v1.6 Profiles

    • Profiles: the game asks for your name the first time you play. Click your profile in the top right of the main menu to see it.
    • Your profile tracks every game you play: level, total accuracy, shots fired, cubes killed, time played and best wave.
    • Levels: every point you score is XP. You'll see a banner mid-fight when you level up.
    • Profile pictures: choose from 3D portraits of things in the game, including every cube, the King, all five guns, a crate, a barrel, a tree and a health pack.

    v1.5.1 Hands on

    • You can now see your arms and hands. Every gun is held properly: trigger finger on the trigger, the other hand on the handguard, pump or forend, and both hands on the pistol.
    • Animated reloads: your free hand pulls the old magazine, grabs a fresh one and pushes it home. On the shotgun it feeds shells in one at a time, and on the sniper your hand works the bolt after every shot.
    • Your gun and arms now stay a sensible size at any field of view, instead of shrinking at wide settings and filling the screen at narrow ones.
    • Two new graphics options. Ultra: sharper image, crisper shadows, thicker grass and a longer view distance. Potato: low resolution, flat colours and no shadows or grass, for slow computers.

    v1.5 Real guns

    • All five weapons rebuilt in detail at real-world size: Glock-style pistol, AR-15 rifle with red-dot sight, pump shotgun with walnut stock, bolt-action sniper with a glass-lensed scope, and a sleeker Extreme Gun.
    • Reflective metal, stippled grips, knurled turrets and wood grain.
    • The pistol slide kicks back, the shotgun pumps and the sniper bolt cycles after every shot.
    • Brass shells eject and clink on the ground; magazines drop out and get swapped when you reload.
    • The game now works fully offline from the downloaded file.

    v1.4 The King arrives

    • New boss: the King Cube storms in on wave 15 and every 5 waves after. Jump over its shockwave. It's worth 10 coins.
    • Radar at the top of the screen shows every cube, enemy shot and health pack near you.
    • Kill combos: chain kills within 2.5 seconds to multiply your score, up to ×5.
    • Cubes sometimes drop health packs (+30 HP). Brutes drop them more often.
    • Switch weapons with the scroll wheel, or press Q to swap back to your last one.
    • End-of-game stats: kills, accuracy, best combo, and a "New best" flag.
    • Balance: fewer Blue Cubes per wave early on, and Brutes arrive in smaller groups.
    • Fix: the game now pauses itself when you switch to another tab or window.

    v1.3 Looks & sound

    • New sky, clouds, mountains, forest, swaying grass and textured buildings.
    • Sound effects, damage numbers, hit markers, recoil, screen shake and a Graphics: Low option.
    • Fix: your gun no longer pokes through walls.

    v1.2 Mouse fix

    • The mouse now locks as soon as you press Play, and the game tells you if your browser blocks it.

    v1.1 New cubes & the village

    • Green, Blue and Brute cubes, each with an intro screen.
    • Houses, trees and cover; coins, the Shop and the Extreme Gun.

    v1.0 First release

    • Four weapons, red cubes, waves and settings.
