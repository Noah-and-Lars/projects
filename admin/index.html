Checks every account for the devices it has been used on, then groups accounts that share a device as one person. Each group's main account is picked by the rule below; the rest are alts.

Device IDs are random IDs the site keeps in each browser since 12 Nov 2026. They aren't hardware IDs. A school, library or family computer makes different people look like one person, so check a group before you ban anyone. Accounts not used since device IDs started can't be linked yet.
