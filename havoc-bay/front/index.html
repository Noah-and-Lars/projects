Mission objectives

  • Clear the guards out of each checkpoint, then stand inside it to take it
  • Take the five checkpoints in order, starting from your own base
  • Hold all five to win, or hold the most when the 30 minutes run out

Five checkpoints lie between the two bases: small, medium, the main base, medium, small. The guards shoot both sides and do not come back.
