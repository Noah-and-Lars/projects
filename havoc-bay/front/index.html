Mission objectives

  • Each side has 600 front strength. Knock it to zero to win, or be the stronger side when the 20 minutes run out
  • Every unit a side loses costs it strength, and holding fewer checkpoints than the enemy drains it every second
  • Shells, bombs and rockets that land in the enemy base wear it down too

Five checkpoints lie between the two bases. Each side starts holding the two nearest its base; the fort in the middle is held by a few neutral guards. Take the fort, then push on into the enemy's checkpoints. The side with more troops and vehicles in a checkpoint takes it. Kills, hits, assists and captures earn spawn points (SP) for tanks, helicopters and jets, and you earn a little all the time, more for each checkpoint held. You can start a ground vehicle at any checkpoint your side holds. Hold Tab for the scoreboard; on the deploy screen, Enter sends you straight back in.
