How to play

Throttle Up / W · Brake Down / S · Lean back Left / A · Lean forward Right / D · Restart at checkpoint R · Pause Esc / P · Mute M

On a phone or tablet: lean buttons bottom-left, brake and gas bottom-right. Hold several at once.

Lean in the air to rotate. Match the angle of the landing slope to keep your speed; land badly and you'll lose it. Lean back on the ground to wheelie, lean forward to keep the front down.

Hit the ground with your head and you'll crash, then restart at the last checkpoint. Finish in the top 2 to unlock the next track.
