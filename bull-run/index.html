Welcome to the Stocks Game

You have $10,000 and a market of twelve companies. Buy low, sell high and build the biggest fortune you can. The calendar runs fast (a trading day every couple of seconds), but prices and news only move every 15 trading days, about every 25 seconds, so you have time to think between moves. Your portfolio is saved in this browser.

  • Pick a stock on the left to see its chart, then Buy or Sell under the chart.
  • Read the news. Headlines move prices: earnings, scandals, booms and the odd crash. Rumours (👂) arrive a day or two early and are right more often than not.
  • Earnings come twice a year. The chart header counts down to each company's next report. Every year you get a report card against the market.
  • Reputation: your best net worth unlocks things for good: crypto at $25,000, half-price back-room deals at $50,000, options (calls and puts) at $100,000.
  • Daily challenge (in the menu): the same market for everyone who plays today, one year long (about 7 minutes). One score, copy it and compare.
  • Some companies pay a dividend twice a year just for holding their shares.
  • Press 🕵️ for the back room: insider tips that always come true, pump-and-dump schemes, a loan shark and a regulator who can be bribed. All of it has consequences. Three convictions and you go to prison for a year.
  • Keys: Space pauses (there is no fast-forward: the market runs at one speed). B S H C pick buy, sell, short or cover, ↑ ↓ change stock, Enter trades.
  • Short selling: bet on a fall. Borrow shares and sell them; cover later to buy them back. If the price rises 50% against you, the broker closes it for you.

The back room

The market is paused while you're in here. Nothing in here is legal. Every deal raises the regulator's suspicion (your heat), and the chance of being caught climbs with it. Lie low and the heat fades. Three convictions and you go to prison.
