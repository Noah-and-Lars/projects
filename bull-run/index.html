Welcome to Bull Run

You have $10,000 and a market of twelve companies. Buy low, sell high and build the biggest fortune you can. The market never sleeps while the game runs, and your portfolio is saved in this browser.

  • Pick a stock on the left to see its chart, then Buy or Sell under the chart.
  • Read the news. Headlines move prices: earnings, scandals, booms and the odd crash. Rumours (👂) arrive a day or two early and are right more often than not.
  • Earnings come every quarter. The chart header counts down to each company's next report.
  • Some companies pay a dividend every quarter just for holding their shares.
  • Speed: Space pauses, 1 2 3 set the speed. B and S switch between buying and selling, ↑ ↓ change stock, Enter trades.
