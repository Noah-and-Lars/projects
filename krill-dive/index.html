How to play

  • Every diver gets the same seven prompts each day, like "Name a songbird".
  • You get 3 seconds to read each prompt, then 25 seconds to answer.
  • One shot: the first answer the game recognises is locked in. Answers it doesn't know don't count against you, so keep typing.
  • Rarer answers score more. Every point sinks your krill 10 m.
  • Versus lobby: make a lobby and share its code (or join an open one). When the host starts, everyone gets the same seven random prompts, and the deepest dive wins.
  • Your daily dive goes on today's leaderboard under your arcade name (practice dives don't). Each day ends at midnight UTC, and its final top three stay on the board as a previous day.
