ENDLESSSHOWROOM

An endless furniture store. Friendly staff by day. Not by night.

How to survive

  • The store never ends. There are no exits. Count the days you survive.
  • Day (6 minutes): the lights are on and staff wander about, harmless. Gather food and build.
  • Night (3 minutes): the lights go down and the staff hunt shoppers. They hit hard.
  • Staff can't climb or jump. Stand on something tall and they can't reach you. Wall yourself in and they'll start breaking the furniture.
  • Pick up furniture and carry it to build towers and forts. Stack it to climb higher.
  • Eat to keep your hunger up. Food restocks every morning. First aid kits heal you.
  • Some days bring events: fog, power cuts, overtime staff, or a calm night.
  • Read the aisle signs (like AISLE E3 · S2) to tell friends where you are.
