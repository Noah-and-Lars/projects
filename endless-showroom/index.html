How to survive

  • The store never ends. There are no exits. Count the days you survive.
  • Day (6 minutes): the lights are on and the staff wander about, harmless. Gather food and build.
  • Night (5 minutes): the lights go out and the staff hunt anyone they see or hear. Break their line of sight and they lose you. A click shoves one over for a moment.
  • Staff climb about a metre at a time, and up shelving and bookcases. Build high, then take your last step away. Furniture stays wherever you put it, even in mid-air.
  • Every 10th night is a Crimson Night: more staff, twice as fast, climbing higher. Every 15th day is a Fog Day: they hunt by day and hit very hard.
  • Hunger runs down; at zero you lose health. Energy goes on sprinting, jumping and carrying; with none you can't sprint and get hungry faster. Health only comes back from food and first aid.
  • Food and first aid restock on Mondays and Fridays. Cafeterias have the most. Carry a lamp at night to see.
  • Whistle to see who's nearby and show your friends where you are, but at night the staff hear it too.
  • Read the aisle signs (like AISLE E3 · S2) to tell friends where you are, and drop waypoints to find your base again.
