    How to survive

    • The store never ends. There are no exits. Count the days you survive.
    • Day (6 minutes): the lights are on and the staff wander about, harmless. Gather food and build.
    • Night (5 minutes, and 4 seconds longer every day): the lights go out and the staff hunt anyone they see or hear. Break their line of sight and they lose you. A click shoves one over for a moment, but each shove costs energy.
    • Staff climb about a metre at a time, and up shelving and bookcases. They can't crouch or open doors, and they see straight through pallets, coffee tables and ladders. Build high and solid.
    • Energy drains all the time, faster when you move or sprint and when you're hurt; only food and drink bring it back. With none you can't sprint or shove and you get hungry faster. Hunger runs down more slowly; at zero you lose health fast. Health only comes back from food and first aid.
    • Food and first aid restock every 2 nights. Cafeterias have the most. Carry a lamp at night to see.
    • Whistle (tap H, or hold it to call for help) so friends see a marker where you are, but at night the staff hear it too.
    • F on a chair, sofa or bed sits you down, and you get your breath back faster. Walk into a ball to kick it.
    • Read the aisle signs (like AISLE E3 · S2) and the screens on the big grid pillars to tell friends where you are, and drop waypoints to find your base again.
    • Hold E to pick furniture up (heavy pieces take longer). Let go touching something and it stays put; let go in mid-air and it drops. F uses things: lamps, light strips, doors, screens, record players, dice, gift boxes, appliances. A bed, sofa or beanbag softens a fall.
