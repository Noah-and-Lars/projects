Controls

        

            
              
Moving

              
WASDMove

              
MouseLook

              
ShiftSprint (hold; double-tap for a tactical sprint)

              
CCrouch; while sprinting, slide (double-tap: onto your back)

              
ZGo prone; while sprinting, dive

              
SpaceJump; at a ledge, climb or vault

            
            
              
The gun

              
Left mouseFire (hold for automatic fire with the MP7A1, AK-74M and AK-74U)

              
Right mouseAim down the sights (hold)

              
QAim: click to toggle, or hold

              
RReload

              
FInspect the gun (not the FN 502, the Hi-Power, the AR-15, the Makarov PM, the AK-74M or the SPAS-12, which have no inspect)

              
1 to 9, 0, - and =, mouse wheelSwitch guns: the PF-9, M1911, Beretta 92FS, FN 502, Browning Hi-Power, Xiri X4, MP7A1, AR-15, Makarov PM, AK-74M, AK-74U and Franchi SPAS-12 (or pick one in the loadout menu)

              
TLight and laser on and off

            
            
              
Menus and more

              
BLoadout: pick a gun, its attachments, and your outfit; and watch the gun breakdowns

              
PPhoto mode: fly round and look at yourself

              
EscPause: the main menu, with Resume at the top
