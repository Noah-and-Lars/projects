# Beat Dash

A one-button rhythm platformer in a single HTML file, in the style of Geometry Dash. Your cube runs forward on its own; you jump over spikes, bounce off pads and orbs, and fly a ship through tight corridors, all in time with the music.

## Play

Download `index.html` and open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. It works offline and on phones and tablets (turn them sideways).

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Space / Up / W, click or tap | Jump (cube), fly up (ship) |
| Hold | Keep jumping as soon as you land |
| Esc / P | Pause |
| R | Restart the level |
| Z / X | Practice mode: place / remove a checkpoint |

## Levels

| Level | Difficulty | Stars | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neon Start | Easy | 2 | Spikes, block stairs, jump pads, orbs and two ship sections |
| Pulse Runner | Normal | 4 | Floating platforms, orb chains, pink pads and a longer ship section |
| Overdrive | Hard | 7 | Faster speed, saw blades, gravity portals, blue orbs and pads, two ship sections |

Each level has its own soundtrack, made in code with Web Audio, and obstacles are placed on the beat. Colors change as you go through a level, and the background and orbs pulse with the music.

## Game pieces

- **Yellow pad**: launches you high. **Pink pad**: a small hop. **Blue pad**: flips gravity.
- **Yellow orb**: tap while touching it to jump again in mid-air. **Pink orb**: a smaller jump. **Blue orb**: flips gravity.
- **Pink portal**: turns you into a ship. **Green portal**: back to a cube.
- **Yellow / blue portal**: gravity up / gravity back down.

## Features

- Normal mode (one life, restart from the start) and practice mode (checkpoints are placed automatically and you can add your own)
- Best % for normal and practice, attempts, jumps and stars are saved in your browser
- Icon kit with 4 cube designs and 12 colors for each of the two icon colors
- Music and sound effects can be turned off in Settings

Every level was checked with a bot that plays the game's own physics and proves each level can be beaten.
